FAQ

Questions clients ask first

Where does AI actually help in marketing?

In the repeat work: research, content drafts, reporting, lead routing and follow-up. It saves time and keeps output consistent. Strategy, judgment and final review stay with people.

Which tools do you work with?

HubSpot, GoHighLevel and Zoho for CRM; n8n, Make and Zapier for automation; GA4, Tag Manager and Looker Studio for analytics; Meta, Google and LinkedIn for ads; and ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini for AI workflows. I recommend tools based on what your business already uses.

How does a project start?

With a short conversation about your goals, current setup and where growth is getting stuck. From there I suggest a clear scope, so you know exactly what will be built and what results to track.

How soon will I see results?

Automations and tracking usually show value within weeks. Acquisition and content results build over a few months, so every project tracks early signals alongside leads and revenue.
