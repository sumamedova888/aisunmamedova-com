How a month works

A steady rhythm, every month

You approve the plan once a month. My team and I handle the rest, and you always know what's coming next.

Week 1 · Plan

I review last month's numbers and set the content calendar, campaigns and priorities. You approve.

Week 2 · Create

My designers produce posts, videos and ad creatives from my briefs. AI speeds up drafts; I review everything.

Week 3 · Run

Publishing, ads, email and community. I watch performance and adjust while campaigns are live.

Week 4 · Report

A clear report: what moved, what didn't, and what changes next month. Then the cycle repeats.

FAQ

Questions clients ask first

What exactly do you handle each month?

All of it: I set the strategy and monthly plan, brief my design team, write and schedule content, run ads and email depending on your plan, and send a monthly report. You approve the plan; you don't manage the work.

Will AI-generated content sound generic?

Not if it's built on a brand voice brief and real examples, and edited by a person. I use AI for drafting and production; opinions, examples and numbers stay yours.

Which platforms do you manage?

Mainly LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok, plus website and AI search content as an add-on. The Social Media Management plan covers up to two platforms; Growth and Full Partner plans add ads, email and more channels.

How much does it cost?

Each plan is a fixed monthly fee based on scope: channels, content volume and ad management. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms. Share your details in the form above and you'll get a one-page proposal within 24 hours of our call.

How soon will I see results?

You'll see consistent, on-brand publishing within the first month. Audience growth and enquiries compound over several months, which is why every report tracks leading signals like saves, shares and profile clicks.
